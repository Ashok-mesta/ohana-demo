Questions

How the demos work and how to share them.

How do I share a demo?

Send the page link, or open a demo and press Copy link in the bottom bar. The link opens that demo full screen on its own, with no sign-in and nothing to install.

What is the difference between Watch and Try it yourself?

Watch plays the demo with a cursor and captions; you can pause, slow it down or restart it. Try it yourself opens the live prototype with a highlighted prompt on the exact element to use next.

Why does every demo open full screen?

A demo on a call should fill the screen. The frame is 16:9 and scales to any window. Press Esc, or Close in the bottom bar, to come back.

Whose data is on screen?

Demo data only. The account is Ashok at ashok@mesta.xyz. Nothing is live and nothing is submitted.
